Kazu Dragons
Support

Kazu Dragons is a math practice game for kindergarten through grade 5. Hatch a dragon, answer math problems, and watch it grow.

  • No ads
  • No in-app purchases
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • Works offline, including in airplane mode

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas? Email justinedwardvolmer@gmail.com. A parent or guardian should send the message.

Common questions

Do I need the internet?
No. The whole game is on your device.

Where is my progress saved?
On your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes the progress. Progress does not move between devices.

What are Scales?
Scales are earned by answering math problems and are spent on colors, patterns and things to wear. They cannot be bought with money.

How do I change the grade?
Tap the Grade button at the top of the screen and pick kindergarten through grade 5. Dragons, Scales and stars stay.

How do I turn read-aloud on or off?
Tap the Read button at the top of the screen. Read-aloud is silent while the ring/silent switch is set to silent.

The game is quiet.
Check the volume and the ring/silent switch on the side of the device.
