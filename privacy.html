Kazu Dragons
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

The short version: Kazu Dragons does not collect any information about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for a name, age, email address, location, photos, contacts or microphone.

What stays on the device

Game progress is saved only on the device the game is played on: the dragons and the names typed for them, math progress, Scales and stars, and settings such as grade and read-aloud. This information never leaves the device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Internet

The game works fully offline and does not need an internet connection.

Read-aloud

Read-aloud uses the voice built into the device. The words are spoken on the device and are not sent anywhere.

Children

Kazu Dragons is made for children and families. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics. It is distributed through the Apple App Store; Apple's own privacy policy covers the store and your Apple account.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
